Privacy Policy: Fruit Drop: Pair Match Puzzle

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Package: com.avy.fruit.match.pair.puzzle.game. Developer: Sandip Vadsak ("we").

Fruit Drop is an offline puzzle game for players aged 13 and older. You do not need an account. Your game progress (level, coins, boosters, settings) is stored only on your device.

Data collected by third-party services

The game uses the services below. They may collect and share device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location (from the IP address), app interactions, purchase history, crash logs and diagnostics, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

  • Google AdMob (ads): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Unity Ads (ad mediation): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Pangle (ad mediation): pangleglobal.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and your choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → PRIVACY OPTIONS. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Uninstalling the game deletes all game data stored on your device.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows local reminders (for example, when a daily reward is ready). They are created on your device and can be turned off in Android settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game offers no real-money gambling, betting or prizes of real-world value.
