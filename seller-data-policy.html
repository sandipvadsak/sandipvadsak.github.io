Seller Data Policy

Applies to the private Amazon Selling Partner API applications used by our own seller accounts. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

1. Scope

Our applications are private tools used only by our own business to manage our own Amazon seller accounts in India. They are not offered to any other seller, customer or third party.

2. What data we collect

  • Order data: order ID, SKU, quantity, ship-by date and shipping/pickup status.
  • Business data: sales, inventory, listing, pricing and settlement reports for our own accounts.
  • Buyer personal information (name, address, phone) may be returned by Amazon only while scheduling a shipment or printing a label. We use it only for that shipment and do not store it.

3. How we use data

  • Schedule Amazon Easy Ship pickups and dispatch our own orders on time.
  • Internal accounting, inventory planning and an internal business dashboard.
  • Internal order alerts to our dispatch staff (order ID and SKU only, no buyer details).

We do not use Amazon data for marketing, do not contact buyers outside Amazon, and do not sell or rent any data.

4. Sharing

We do not share Amazon information with any outside party.

5. Storage and security

  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.
  • Business data is stored in a managed cloud database with provider-managed encryption at rest and row-level access rules.
  • API keys and passwords are kept in local configuration files and encrypted cloud secrets, never in source code or public repositories.
  • Access to systems and keys is limited to the business owner. Staff have no system access.
  • Accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification.

6. Retention and disposal

  • Buyer personal information: not stored. If any is held temporarily, it is deleted within 30 days of order delivery.
  • Order and business records (no buyer details) are kept for accounting and deleted when no longer needed.
  • Backups are encrypted and kept on separate drives; old backups are deleted on a rolling basis.

7. Security incidents

If we suspect unauthorised access or a data leak, we immediately revoke and rotate API keys, stop affected tools, investigate logs, and report incidents involving Amazon information to security@amazon.com within 24 hours of detection.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the account owner through Amazon Seller Central.
